Current Crisis 2024-2026

2024

Civil War & Forced Conscription

Arakan Army (AA) vs. Military Junta

Fighting between the ethnic Rakhine Arakan Army and Myanmar's military junta intensified dramatically in Rakhine State. The conflict has trapped Rohingya civilians between two armed forces, with both sides committing abuses.

Forced Military Conscription

The military junta began forcibly conscripting Rohingya men into military service. This represents a new phase of exploitation, using Rohingya as cannon fodder in the junta's losing war against ethnic armed organizations.

New Refugee Wave

Between 65,000 and 70,000+ new Rohingya refugees crossed into Bangladesh, overwhelming already strained resources in Cox's Bazar camps. Many arrived with severe injuries and trauma.

Naf River Massacre (August 2024)

Over 200 Rohingya were killed in a massacre at the Naf River, which forms the border between Myanmar and Bangladesh. Survivors reported that armed groups fired on boats attempting to cross, killing men, women, and children.

Key Statistics for 2024

  • 65,000-70,000+ new refugees to Bangladesh
  • 200+ killed in Naf River massacre
  • Thousands killed in broader conflict
  • 600,000 Rohingya remain trapped in Rakhine
  • Complete collapse of humanitarian access in conflict zones
2025-2026

Current Status - Ongoing Emergency

Refugee Camp Conditions

The Cox's Bazar refugee camps remain the world's largest refugee settlement, housing 1,133,981 registered refugees as of April 2025. Conditions continue to deteriorate:

  • Severe overcrowding with 40,000+ people per square kilometer
  • Limited access to clean water and sanitation
  • Malnutrition rates rising, especially among children
  • Fire risks due to flammable shelter materials
  • Flood and landslide risks during monsoon season

Boat Crisis Surge

Desperate conditions have driven a massive increase in dangerous sea journeys:

  • 3,100 attempted sea journeys between January and May 2025
  • 457 people died or went missing at sea in this period
  • Total dead or missing since 2022: 1,878
  • Smugglers charge $2,000-$4,000 per person for dangerous trips
  • Many boats are abandoned at sea by traffickers

Food Security Crisis

The World Food Programme has been forced to cut rations due to funding shortages:

  • Food rations reduced from $12 to $6 per person per month
  • Rising malnutrition among children under 5
  • Increased child marriage and child labor as families struggle
  • Reports of families selling assets to buy food

Repatriation Completely Stalled

All attempts at voluntary repatriation have failed:

  • Myanmar refuses to guarantee safety and citizenship rights
  • Rohingya refuse to return without guarantees of security
  • International community lacks leverage over Myanmar junta
  • China and India maintain ties with junta, undermining pressure

International Response Gaps

Despite international attention, meaningful action remains limited:

  • UN Security Council blocked by veto powers
  • ASEAN "Five-Point Consensus" completely ignored by junta
  • Humanitarian funding critically short ($876M needed, far less received)
  • No accountability for perpetrators of genocide
PRIORITY NEEDS

Critical Humanitarian Needs

Immediate Needs

  • Food Security: Adequate nutrition for 1.1M+ refugees
  • Healthcare: Medical facilities, maternal health, mental health
  • Education: Schools for 500,000+ children
  • Protection: Gender-based violence prevention, child protection
  • Shelter: Fire-resistant, weather-proof housing

Long-Term Solutions

  • Safe and voluntary repatriation with citizenship rights
  • Accountability for genocide and crimes against humanity
  • Regional resettlement and third-country pathways
  • Restoration of Rohingya rights in Myanmar
  • Sustainable development in Rakhine State
