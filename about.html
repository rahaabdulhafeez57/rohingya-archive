About This Archive

Our mission, methodology, and sources

Our Mission

To document, preserve, and disseminate the historical record of the Rohingya people. This archive serves as an educational resource for researchers, journalists, policymakers, and the global public to understand the full scope of Rohingya history—from ancient settlement to present-day crisis.

Why This Archive Exists

The Rohingya people have faced systematic erasure of their history, identity, and rights. The Myanmar government has consistently denied their existence as an indigenous ethnic group, destroyed historical records, and prevented independent documentation.

This archive exists to:

  • Preserve historical truth against deliberate erasure
  • Provide verified, sourced information for education
  • Support advocacy for Rohingya rights
  • Document the ongoing crisis for future accountability
  • Ensure the Rohingya story is not forgotten

Methodology & Sources

All content in this archive is compiled from verified, authoritative sources including:

  • United Nations: UNHCR, OHCHR, UN Fact-Finding Mission reports
  • International Organizations: IOM, WFP, ICC, ICJ documentation
  • Human Rights Groups: Human Rights Watch, Amnesty International, Fortify Rights
  • Medical Organizations: MSF (Doctors Without Borders) mortality surveys
  • Academic Research: Yale, Queen Mary University, International State Crime Initiative
  • News Organizations: BBC, Al Jazeera, Reuters investigative reports

We prioritize primary sources, official reports, and peer-reviewed research. Where figures vary between sources, we present the range or the most conservative verified estimate.

Content Principles

  • Accuracy: All facts are cross-referenced with multiple sources
  • Objectivity: We document events without political bias
  • Respect: We treat victims and survivors with dignity
  • Transparency: Sources are cited and verifiable
  • Updates: Content is regularly updated as new information emerges

Limitations & Disclaimers

This is an educational documentation project. We are not affiliated with any government, political party, or armed group. The archive does not:

  • Accept funding from governments or political organizations
  • Promote violence or hatred against any group
  • Claim copyright over historical facts
  • Replace official humanitarian or legal processes

Figures on deaths, displacements, and refugees are estimates based on the best available data. Actual numbers may be higher due to underreporting and restricted access.

How to Use This Archive

This website is designed for:

  • Students & Researchers: Use the timeline and history sections for academic work
  • Journalists: Reference verified facts and statistics
  • Advocates: Access documentation for campaigns and awareness
  • Educators: Use as teaching material on human rights and genocide
  • General Public: Learn about one of the world's most urgent humanitarian crises
