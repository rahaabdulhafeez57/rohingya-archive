Who Are the Rohingya?

The Rohingya are a stateless ethnic group, predominantly Muslim, indigenous to Rakhine State (historically Arakan) in Myanmar. For centuries, they lived alongside Buddhist communities in the region. Following repeated cycles of targeted violence since 1962 and continuous denial of their rights, nearly one million Rohingya now live in the world's largest refugee camp in Cox's Bazar, Bangladesh.
